Little things that help

  • Sign in only if you want toEnter your email and a 6-digit code to keep favourite cafés, favourite orders and loyalty stamps on every device.
  • Your usual, rememberedSet defaults such as oat milk or decaf, and they’re picked for you each time.
  • Allergens that follow youTurn on your allergens in Settings and menus flag what to avoid. The café is told with your order.
  • Loyalty stampsCafés can run a stamp card. It shows as “5/8 Stamps Collected”, with a free drink at the end.
  • Something wrong?Every completed order has a Support button: ask for help, report a problem, or request a refund with photos.

For cafés

Take pre-orders and payments without building your own app. Your logo and colours lead inside your menu; Docket stays in the background.

The kitchen screen

A new order rings a loud chime and keeps nagging until someone accepts it. Tickets read from two metres away on a tablet.

New → Preparing → Ready → Done
  • Never miss an orderChime, push alerts and optional Star thermal-printer tickets.
  • Stay in control in a rushPause new orders or add 10 minutes to prep time with one tap, per branch.
  • Scheduled orders stay quietThey appear at pickup time minus your prep time, not at 7am for a 1pm lunch.
  • Several sitesBranches have their own hours, addresses and menu availability under one account.

Set up your way

  • Your brand, your menuLogo, brand colour, categories, options and extras, allergens and dietary tags, special hours.
  • Offers and loyaltyDiscount codes, plus a stamp card: a stamp per order or per item, rewarding a discount or a free product.
  • Refunds that fitRefund single items or the whole order. Customer refund requests land in a Disputes page, and you have 7 days to respond.
  • Practise firstTest mode lets you place fake orders from your own store without paying.
  • Get foundPrint a QR poster that opens your menu. Reviews and your food hygiene rating are shown to customers.
  • Real helpEmail merchantsupport@placemyorder.online. An AI assistant that knows Docket answers straight away, and a person steps in when it can’t help.

Money, plainly

Customers never pay a Docket surcharge. Cafés pay 5% of each paid order, taken from the payout by Mollie. Mollie’s own card fees are separate.

A £10.00 order

Customer pays£10.00
Docket fee, 5%−£0.50
Café receives£9.50

Before Mollie’s card processing fees. Refer another café and you both pay 2.5% for 30 days.

If you’d rather keep the full £10.00

Cafés can switch on “off-hand service fee to customer”. Prices go up by just enough to cover the fee. It’s the price that changes, not an added charge.

Menu price you set£10.00
Shown to customer£10.53
Café receives£10.00

Prices on Docket can never be more than 7.5% above in-store prices.

Held to a standard

Docket checks the Food Standards Agency’s public hygiene ratings automatically. Food businesses rated 3 or below are not allowed on the platform, and are hidden from customers until their rating is 4 or 5.

Cafés agree to our Merchant Terms before they launch, including the 7.5% price rule. If a customer asks for a refund and the café doesn’t reply within 7 days, the refund is granted automatically.
